Our Approach

01 / Why

Bridging the gap between theory and practice.

We believe students should have the opportunity to explore quantitative finance beyond the classroom and turn academic knowledge into practical experience.

02 / How

Learning through collaboration and innovation.

By bringing together students with an interest in mathematics, programming and financial markets to exchange ideas, develop skills and work on quantitative projects.

03 / What

Research. Develop. Test.

We explore quantitative finance through financial modelling, academic research, programming and the development and backtesting of systematic investment strategies.

About us

Three teams. One fund.

Stockholm Student Quantitative Fund is run by students of KTH who apply mathematics, statistics and software to real markets. Research & Trading develops, backtests and executes strategies, Development builds the systems that run them, and Marketing & Sales connects the fund with students, partners and sponsors. The Board leads the fund and sets its direction.
