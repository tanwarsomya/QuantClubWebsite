KTH Quantitative
Investment Club

KTH Quantitative Investment Club is a student-run fund at KTH Royal Institute of Technology, applying mathematics, statistics and software to real markets. It is organised in four teams: Research develops and backtests strategies, Risk keeps the portfolio within its limits, Development builds the systems that run it, and Marketing connects the club with students and industry.
