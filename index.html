Stockholm Student
Quantitative Fund

Stockholm Student Quantitative Fund is run by students of KTH Royal Institute of Technology who apply mathematics, statistics and software to real markets. The fund is organised in four teams: Research develops and backtests strategies, Trading & Risk executes them and keeps the portfolio within its limits, Development builds the systems that run it, and Marketing & Sales connects the fund with students, partners and sponsors.
